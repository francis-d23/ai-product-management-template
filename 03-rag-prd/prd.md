## Problem & user

RocketShip PMs need this handled: Juno will review reported support related issues and create a priority list that is generated daily for the Product Manager to Review. Juno does this inside limits a human set on purpose, and defends every call it makes.

## 01 Context · Data Requirements

**Required sources, and exclusions**

Required: RocketShip Strategy One-Pager (the M2 deliverable, the single authority) + Servicedesk tickets tagged P0/P1/P2 from the last 90 days + MS Teams #voice-of-customer from the last 90 days.

Excluded: Salesforce closed-lost notes and exec MS Teams DMs. Both are opinion-heavy and would let Juno rank on seniority rather than evidence. Named here so the exclusion is a decision on the record, not an oversight.

**How fresh must it be**

Anything older than 48 hours is serviced without a "Potentially Stale" label.  If the strategy document is unreachable, Juno does not fall back to cached copy or make its own judgement.  It will return "No Strategy Source available" and ranks nothing.

**Deliberate omissions**

No notify_stakeholder() or post_to_MS Teams(). Both are class SEND. A wrong priority in a draft costs a PM two minutes of reading. The same wrong priority broadcast to #product costs a week of politics, and you cannot unsend it.

No delete_ticket() or close_ticket(). Destructive, and nothing about prioritisation requires it.

## 03 Loop · AI Costs & Latency

**Turn ceiling and escalation**

5 passes per batch for now

**Latency and cost target**

p95 under 15 seconds from Process click to ranked draft. Cost ceiling $0.18 per run.

At roughly 40 runs per PM per month across 4 PMs, that is about $29 a month. Small enough that the turn ceiling is really a latency decision, not a cost one, and worth saying so out loud so nobody optimises the wrong number.

## 04 Memory · Data Requirements

**What persists, at what scope**

Per PM, per sprint. Juno remembers the rationale behind each ranking it produced this sprint, so it can explain a change rather than silently re-ranking.

Nothing crosses between teams. Team A's prioritisation rationale must never surface in Team B's session, because their strategy documents differ and the confusion would be invisible.

**Expiry and write rules**

TTL of one sprint on model-generated rationale, expiring with the sprint boundary.

PM corrections persist 90 days and outrank stored model output on every future run. If a PM overrode a ranking, Juno leads with that override and explains that a human disagreed with it, rather than quietly re-deriving its original answer.

## 05 Permissions · AI Risks & Mitigations

**The permission tier**

| Side-effect class | Tier |
|---|---|
| read | auto |
| draft | auto |
| write | confirm |
| send | blocked |

**Justification**

Read is auto: the blast radius is wasted tokens.

Draft is auto: a proposed ranking sitting in a review panel has harmed nobody. Making drafts confirm would mean a click per insight, which is the approval-fatigue failure, and PMs would start clicking through without reading.

Write is confirm: write_roadmap() changes shared state the whole team plans against. Recoverable in git, but a colleague may act on it before anyone notices, so a human sees the citation before it lands.

Send is blocked in V1: nothing Juno produces reaches anyone outside the PM reviewing it. Revisit only once the Module 6 evals give a real precision number.

## 06 Verification · AI Testing & Measurement

**The check before output ships**

Every priority must cite at least one clause that appears verbatim in the strategy document loaded this session. A paraphrase fails the check.

Juno may not assume, make up, reconstruct, or improve a clause to pass the gate. If it cannot find support, the correct output is an honest "Cannot Verify", not a better-sounding citation.

**Failure behaviour**

Unverified priorities are shown to the PM with an "Cannot Verify" badge and the line "No supporting clause found." They are never silently dropped, because a quietly missing insight is invisible and an unverified one is a prompt to check.

An unverified priority may be drafted but may NOT be written to the roadmap: write_roadmap() rejects it and says why.

If more than half the priorities in a run come back unverified, banner the whole result: the strategy document is probably missing, stale, or the wrong one.

Golden set, thresholds, and regression cadence land in Module 6.
